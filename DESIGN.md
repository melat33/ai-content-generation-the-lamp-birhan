# Design: audience, intent, user tests

## Audience and intent (written before generating)
**Who:** young Ethiopians (students, graduates, diaspora), watching on a phone, vertical.
**Intent:** pride in what Ethiopia built, a sense of loss when learning stopped being honoured, and a question to carry away:
*what if we valued those who think, teach and build?*
**Thread:** a brass oil lamp and an open book, passed through every era.
**Approach:** the decline is posed as a question, not a verdict (historians debate causes; the imperial system's failures also fed the 1974 revolution).
Historical figures appear only symbolically, never as realistic faces. Hardship is shown without blaming any group.
**Define ("how might we"):** How might we make young Ethiopians feel the value of learned people, in 75 seconds, without a history lecture?
